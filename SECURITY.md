# Security reports

Report vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/inline-chat/inline/security/advisories/new).

Do not include vulnerabilities, credentials or private conversations in public issues or pull requests.

You can reach out inside the app to @Mo to discuss this in private with us. After your report is accepted we'll keep in touch and notify you when it's fixed. We'll attribute you for issues with real impact. For normal missing features and security measures of a beta that pose no immediate threat or personal data leak, we may not position it as "a security incident" and only include it in our changelog with attributing you as the person suggesting the fix or improvement.
