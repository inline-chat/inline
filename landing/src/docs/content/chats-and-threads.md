Home or a space provides the context. A chat holds the conversation. Your sidebar or Open list is the set of conversations you are working with now.

## Conversation Types

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Direct message | A conversation with another person or a bot. |
| Thread | A focused conversation with messages and an optional title. It can live in Home or a space. |
| Subthread | A conversation beneath another thread. |
| Reply thread | A subthread anchored to a particular message. Open it through that message's reply count. |

For example, keep a launch discussion in one thread and discuss a particular screenshot in its reply thread. Start a separate private thread if the new discussion needs a narrower audience.

## Who Can Read a Thread?

| Thread | Access boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Home thread | Access is granted to participants; Home threads cannot be public. |
| Private space thread | Requires thread access. Being in the same space alone does not grant access. |
| Public space thread | Treat the content as internet-accessible, not confidential or restricted to your team. |
| Subthread or reply thread | Inherits access from its parent. Direct participants may also have access to the child. |

A child conversation is not automatically more private than its parent. Check the participants and visibility before adding someone or sharing content. A link points to a conversation; it does not itself grant permission to read a private chat.

Bots and agents are participants too. Connecting one can send accessible content to its operator or model provider. Read [Security](/docs/security#chats-and-integrations) and the integration's access policy before using confidential material.

## Open, Follow, and Notifications

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Open / Close | Whether a chat belongs to your active working set. Close does not delete messages or remove other people's access. |
| Follow / Unfollow | Whether normal thread activity should surface for you. Mentions and replies can still surface when unfollowed. |
| Notifications | Your notification preference; separate from access and Open membership. Choosing per-thread **All** notifications also enables **Follow**. |

To clear your working set, close a chat and reopen it when needed. To change interruptions, review notification settings. To change who can read a conversation, review access and participants; neither closing nor unfollowing changes that boundary.
